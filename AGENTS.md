# Social platform logo changes

Before changing third-party social platform logo assets or their rendering, read and follow [the social platform logo usage guide](frontend/assets/brand/social/USAGE.md).

Apply that guide only to social platform marks. Do not apply its platform-specific rules to GoodMarketing's own logo or customer-uploaded company logos. Preserve the approved LinkedIn reference identified in the guide unless the user explicitly requests a replacement.

LinkedIn must have no added backing, white or otherwise. Preserve the blue mark and white “in” lettering as artwork, with transparency outside its silhouette. Do not restore the earlier white CSS backing treatment.

## CI/CD and deployment

For CI/CD, deployment, or release verification, read and follow the
[GoodMorning.mx CI/CD workflow](CI-CD.md). Check CI in GitHub after changes
reach `dev` and verify the automated deployment for the same commit in Coolify.
