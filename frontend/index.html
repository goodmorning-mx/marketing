GoodMarketing is a complete toolkit for managing your marketing and public relations, with coordinated publishing across your social platforms throughout a campaign.

From first thought to next idea

A campaign is a journey, not a pile of tasks.

The platform's purpose is to connect the steps so a team can see what's happening, what's waiting, and what comes next.

Posts across platforms throughout the full campaign lifecycle.
